# Daily updates — guide for agents and managers

## Signing in

Open the tracker link from the content team.

- **Sign in with company Google account** (if your team uses it) — use the same email you gave in the Google Form.
- Or use the **one-time setup link** the content team sent you: choose your own password (at least 10 characters,
  not your name, email or Instagram handle). After that, sign in with your email and that password.

Forgot your password or link expired? Ask the content team for a new setup link.
We will never ask for your Instagram password.

## Every working day

1. Open the tracker (it works on your phone — you can add it to your home screen).
2. Under **Today**, choose:
   - **Posted** → paste the direct link to your post or reel. In Instagram tap **⋯ / Share → Copy link** on the post.
     It looks like `https://www.instagram.com/reel/…` or `https://www.instagram.com/p/…`.
   - **Not posted** → write a short reason (e.g. “Client viewings all day”).
3. Tap **Submit update**. You'll see the date the content is for and the time you submitted.

Please update **before 7:00 PM (Dubai time)**. After 7:00 PM a missing update is flagged for the content team to
follow up with you.

## Missed a day?

The next working day you'll see **Previous working day** with a form. You can still add that update — it will be
marked **Submitted late**. Older days can't be changed by you; contact the content team.

## Made a mistake?

Open **Correct this update** under the day, change it and save. Your previous answer is kept in the history, so
corrections are visible to the content team.

## Privacy

You can only see your own profile and updates. Other agents can't see yours.
